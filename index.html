About Me

For the past three years, I’ve been creating content consistently. It started as a hobby, making mostly gym videos, but everything changed in 2025. I began documenting my self improvement journey and speaking openly about the battles I’ve faced with addiction, depression, and identity. Sharing my story connected me with thousands of people. More importantly, it built a community. Every day, people message me telling me how my content helped them quit their own addictions or change the direction of their lives. That impact means everything to me, because there was a time when I didn’t believe I would ever escape the path I was on. Choosing to embody dedication is my way of becoming the light for anyone going through their own dark moments. Through my coaching, I help young men rebuild themselves from the ground up — developing structure, integrating discipline, and cultivating a clear sense of purpose. My goal isn’t to motivate people for a moment. My goal is to give them the tools to create lasting change and become someone they’re proud of.
